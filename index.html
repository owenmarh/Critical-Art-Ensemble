Critical Art Ensemble

The Electronic Disturbance

Chapters 1 + 2

THE FIRST PARAGRAPH:

"The rules of cultural and political resistance have dramatically changed. The revolution in technology brought about by the rapid development of the computer and video has created a new geography of power relations in the first world that could only be imagined as little as twenty years ago: people are reduced to data, surveillance occurs on a global scale, minds are melded to screenal reality, and an authoritarian power emerges that thrives on absence. The new geography is a virtual geography, and the core of political and cultural resistance must assert itself in this electronic space." (3).

IN THE PAGES THAT FOLLOW, CRITICAL ART ENSEMBLE WALKS US THROUGH AN EVOLUTION OF WHAT THEY CALL 'ELITE POWER' FROM THE SEDENTARY TO THE NOMADIC. THE AUTHORS MEAN NOMADIC NOT ONLY IN THE MATERIAL SENSE BUT ALSO THE VIRTUAL (PLACELESSNESS BEING AN INTRINSIC CHARACTERISTIC OF THE LATTER REALM).

Arguing the modern form of elite power as evolved from its previously sedentary state, CAE notes that, "having rid itself of its national and urban bases to wander in absence on the electronic pathways, [elite power] can no longer be disrupted by strategies predicated upon the contestation of sedentary forces" (23). Meanwhile, the authors note, "The architectural monuments of power are hollow and empty, and function now only as bunkers for the complicit and those who acquiesce...secure places...[that] silence resistance and resentment by the signs of resolution, continuity, commodification, and nostalgia" (23).

KEY TO CAE'S TEXT IS THE BUNKER.

CAE makes the analogy of the web to the street to the web as the connective tissue between and around the bunkers. In this marginalized public network, those who occupy the street are socially constructed as liabilities to the safety of those who seek (the illusion) of security in the bunkers. Yet, "the price of this protectionism" -- that is, the illusion of safety bunker occupants are communicated -- "is the relinquishment of individual sovereignty...[acting] as a seductive agent offering the credible illusion of consumptive choice and ideological peace for the complicit or...an aggressive force demanding acquiescence for the resistance [to it/elite power]" (28).

AS LONG AS THE GENERAL POPULACE AGREES TO PLAY THE ROLE OF THE CONSUMER, THEY WILL SEE THOSE WHO CANNOT CONSUME -- THOSE WITHOUT MONEY, THOSE WHO ARE OTHERWISE MARGINALIZED -- AS A DANGER TO THEIR FREEDOM (TO CONSUME).

WHEN CAE SPEAKS OF BUNKERS, THEY ARE SPEAKING OF THE PHYSICAL AND VIRTUAL INSTITUTIONS THAT CONFINE OUR PARTICIPATION IN THE WORLD TO ONLY BEING ABLE TO ACT AS CONSUMERS. AN ARGUMENT CAN BE MADE THAT MOST PHYSICAL AND VIRTUAL SPACES HAVE BEEN FULLY COLONIZED BY ELITE POWER. WHAT ALL OF THIS COMES DOWN TO IS THE IDEA THAT OUR LIVES MATTER ONLY IF WE CAN AFFORD TO PAY FOR THEM.

THEN, CAE NOTES THAT THE PROBLEM WITH THE NOMADIC ELITE IS ITS SOURCE IS NOT CLEAR.

"How can a subject be critically assessed that cannot be located, examined, or even seen? Subjectively there is a feeling of oppression, and yet it is difficult to locate, let alone assume, an oppressor. In all likelihood, this group is not a class at all -- that is, an aggregate of people with common political and economic interests -- but a downloaded elite military consciousness. The cyberelite is now a transcendent entity that can only be imagined" (18).

WHAT DOES THIS MEAN?

Class = "An aggregate of people with common political and economic interests"

(Modern) Elite Power = "a downloaded elite military consciousness..now a transcendent entity that can only be imagined"

THE PROBLEM IS THAT ELITE POWER'S PLACELESSNESS IS IMMOBILIZING.

"The development of an absent and potentially unassailable nomadic power, coupled with the rear vision of revolution in ruins, has nearly muted the contestational voice" (18).

AFTER ASSESSING A PLURALITY OF ACTIVIST RESPONSES TO THE SEDENTARY AND NOMADIC FORMS OF ELITE POWER, CAE CONCLUDES REPEATEDLY THAT THESE STRATEGIES ARE NOT SUCCESSFUL.

IN ORDER TO UNDERSTAND WHAT CONSTITUTES ELITE POWER IN THE PRESENT, WE NEED TO UNDERSTAND WHAT IT IS RESPONSIBLE FOR. IN OTHER WORDS, WHAT IT HAS BUILT.

"Bunkers have already been described as privatized public spaces which serve various particularized functions, such as political continuity (government offices or national monuments), or areas for consumption frenzy (malls). In line with the feudal tradition of the fortress mentality, the bunker guarantees safety and familiarity in exchange for the relinquishment of individual sovereignty. It can act as a seductive agent offering the credible illusion of consumptive choice and ideological peace for the complicit, or it can act as an aggressive force demanding acquiescence for the resistant. The bunker brings nearly all to its interior with the exception of those left to guard the streets. After all, nomadic power does not offer the choice not to work or not to consume. The bunker is such an all-embracing feature of everyday life that even the most resistant cannot always approach it critically. Alienation, in part, stems from this uncontrollable entrapment in the bunker" (27-28).

"Bunkers vary in appearance as much as they do in function. The nomadic bunker—the product of “the global village”—has both an electronic and an architectural form. The electronic form is witnessed as media; as such it attempts to colonize the private residence. Informative distraction flows in an unceasing stream of fictions produced by Hollywood, Madison Avenue, and CNN. The economy of desire can be safely viewed through the familiar window of screenal space. Secure in the electronic bunker, a life of alienated autoexperience (a loss of the social) can continue in quiet acquiescence and deep privation. The viewer is brought to the world, the world to the viewer, all mediated through the ideology of the screen. This is virtual life in a virtual world" (28).

WHAT IS THE BUNKER?

In its electronic form = media

In its physical form = "privatized public spaces which serve various particularized functions, such as political continuity (government offices or national monuments) or areas for consumption frenzy"

WHAT HAPPENS IN THE BUNKER?

Safety and security in exchange for no more individual autonomy/freedom

Aggression against you unless you agree to consume

WHAT HAPPENS OUTSIDE THE BUNKER?

Those who cannot work or consume live.

CAE ENDS WITH THE PROVOCATION:

"IN ANY BUNKER (ALONG WITH ITS ASSOCIATED GEOGRAPHY, TERRITORY, AND ECOLOGY) THE RESISTANT CULTURAL PRODUCER CAN BEST ACHIEVE DISTURBANCE. THERE IS ENOUGH CONSUMER TECHNOLOGY AVAILABLE TO AT LEAST TEMPORARILY REINSCRIBE THE BUNKER WITH IMAGE AND LANGUAGE THAT REVEAL ITS SACRIFICIAL INTENT, AS WELL AS THE OBSCENITY OF ITS BOURGEOIS UTILITARIAN AESTHETIC. NOMADIC POWER HAS CREATED PANIC IN THE STREETS, WITH ITS MYTHOLOGIES OF POLITICAL SUBVERSION, ECONOMIC DETERIORATION, AND BIOLOGICAL INFECTION, WHICH IN TURN PRODUCE A FORTRESS IDEOLOGY, AND HENCE A DEMAND FOR BUNKERS. IT IS NOW NECESSARY TO BRING PANIC INTO THE BUNKER, THUS DISTURBING THE ILLUSION OF SECURITY AND LEAVING NO PLACE TO HIDE. THE INCITEMENT OF PANIC IN ALL SITES IS THE POSTMODERN GAMBLE" (30.)
